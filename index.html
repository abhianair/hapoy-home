What you get

Built around one question: what's near me?

No feeds to curate, no cities to browse one by one — just what's happening within reach.

Radius that's actually yours

Choose 1, 3, 5, 10, 25 or 50 km — from the map or your account — and the feed follows.

Search any address

Don't want to use your live location? Search a neighbourhood, town or landmark and centre there instead.

Browse as a guest

No account needed to see what's on. Sign in only when you want to save something to your list.

Running a sale or a community program? List it as an organiser — no fees, no limits.
